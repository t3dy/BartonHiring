# Chat-ready Barton Action Points

## Summary for Matthew and Evan
The website work should center on a playful but practical quote experience. The customer should be able to quickly choose a route, describe the property, select key items, and get a rough estimate without feeling like they are filling out a long form.

## Core action points
- Build a simple entry choice: contact us vs. build your move.
- Use a small set of area tiles for common Austin-area routes and a custom-location fallback.
- Show apartment vs. house visually with a gamified feel.
- Collect the details that matter for moving complexity: stairs, elevator, parking, bedrooms, stories, and specialty items.
- Add a box estimator and a crew-size recommendation so the estimate feels realistic.
- Support partner discounts and codes for apartment complexes.
- Keep the branding aligned with the site and the overall Barton moving experience.

## Growth-side action points
- Pair the website flow with review automation and referral incentives.
- Use short-form video and local SEO content to drive leads.
- Make the quote experience the front door for both customer conversion and ongoing marketing.
